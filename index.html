How Electricity.Bill Works

Manage electricity bills, calculate everyone's share, and keep your records organized in one simple place.

Step 01

Add Your Bill

Enter the bill date, month, total amount, and the electricity units used by each person.

Step 02

Automatic Calculation

The system calculates the cost per unit and automatically determines how much each person needs to pay.

Step 03

Keep Everything Organized

Every bill is saved as a card so you can easily review, delete, backup, or restore your records whenever you need them.
